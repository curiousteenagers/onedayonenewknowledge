History

The Great Emu War

The bizarre conflict where thousands of emus outmatched the Australian military.

Key Concepts

  • Royal Australian Artillery: The military unit deployed to fight the emu population, though its organization and history are not discussed.
  • Soldier Settlement Scheme: A government program that granted farmland to World War I veterans, without explaining the specific requirements or administration of the scheme.
  • Rabbit-Proof Fences: Large barriers originally built to stop rabbits, which were easily crossed by migrating emus, though their engineering and full history are not explored.
  • Guerrilla Tactics: A comparison used to describe how emus instinctively avoided military attacks, without discussing the formal principles of guerrilla warfare.
  • Bounty System: A reward program that encouraged civilians to hunt emus after the military campaign failed, though its financial administration is not explained.

Vocabulary

Big Question

"If advanced military technology can be defeated by the instincts of wildlife, what does that reveal about the limits of human control over nature?"

Questions to Explore

  • What if farming systems were designed to work with native animal migration instead of trying to block it?
  • Could the cooperative behavior of emu flocks inspire more efficient decentralized communication networks?
  • How might history have been different if governments had focused on ecological solutions instead of military ones?
