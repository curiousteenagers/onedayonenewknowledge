Philosophy

Absurdism/h1>

The philosophy that explores humanity's search for meaning in a meaningless universe.

Key Concepts

  • Streetcar Commute: A metaphor for the repetitive routines of everyday life that can suddenly be questioned when a person asks, "Why am I doing this?"
  • Cosmic Silence: The idea that the universe offers no inherent answers or purpose, creating the tension at the heart of Camus' philosophy of the absurd.
  • Philosophical Suicide: Albert Camus' term for escaping the absurd by accepting comforting beliefs or illusions instead of confronting reality.
  • Metaphysical Rebellion: The conscious decision to embrace life's lack of inherent meaning while continuing to live with freedom and purpose.
  • Human Solidarity: The connection between people who recognize that everyone faces the same existential questions and shared human condition.

Vocabulary

Lucidity (C2) The quality of thinking clearly and understanding things accurately.
Surmount (C2) To successfully overcome a difficulty or obstacle.
Defiant (C1) Openly resisting authority or refusing to give in.
Vibrant (C1) Full of energy, life, and enthusiasm.
Empathetic (C1) Able to understand and share another person's feelings.

Big Question

"If life has no predetermined meaning, does that give us complete freedom to create our own purpose, or does it leave us permanently searching for one?"

Questions to Explore

  • How would society change if more people embraced uncertainty instead of searching for absolute answers?
  • Could accepting the absurd make us happier than believing life must have a fixed purpose?
  • If meaning is something we create rather than discover, what kind of life would you choose to build?
