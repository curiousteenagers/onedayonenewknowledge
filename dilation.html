Time Dilation

Why time flows differently at extreme speeds and in strong gravity.

Key Concepts

  • Inertial Frame: Mentioned as the state that changes when an astronaut fires their engines to turn around, but the physical definition of a frame of reference is not explained.
  • Atomic Clocks: Cited as the ultra-precise tools used in the Hafele-Keating experiment, but the internal mechanics of how atoms are used to measure time are not detailed.
  • Lorentz Factor: Introduced as the mathematical Greek letter gamma used to calculate time stretching, though the actual algebraic derivation of this formula is skipped.
  • Gravitational Well: Described as a region where gravity is stronger and time moves slower, but the specific geometric curvature of spacetime that creates this "well" is not explored.
  • Primordial Black Holes: Briefly mentioned as potentially forming at the start of the universe, but their unique properties compared to stellar black holes are not explained.

Big Question

"If time is a flexible fabric that stretches based on speed and mass, is there any such thing as a 'now' that the entire universe can agree upon?"

Questions to Explore

  • Could we use time dilation to effectively travel to the edge of the galaxy within a single human lifetime?
  • What would happen to our social structures if people on different planets experienced decades while Earth only experienced days?
  • If time stops at the speed of light, does a photon experience its entire journey across the universe as happening in a single instant?
