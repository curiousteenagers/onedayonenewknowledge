History

Why Do Bank Runs Happen?

Discover how fear and a lack of confidence can cause large numbers of people to withdraw their money from a bank at the same time.

Key Concepts

  • Lidar Technology: An airborne laser-mapping method that can create detailed images of the ground beneath dense forest vegetation, helping researchers discover previously hidden archaeological sites.
  • Law of Environmental Limitation: An outdated anthropological idea that tropical rainforests could not support large and complex populations, a view challenged by archaeological discoveries.
  • Terminal Classic Period: The period from roughly the eighth to the tenth century when many Maya cities experienced political and social changes rather than a single sudden collapse.
  • Fatal Interdependence: The idea that interconnected political and economic systems can cause problems in one region to spread to other dependent communities.
  • Forest Gardening: A sustainable agricultural approach that combines different plants within forest ecosystems while working with local environmental conditions.

Big Question

"If the Maya civilization did not simply disappear but transformed and adapted to changing conditions, how should we redefine what it means for a civilization to collapse?"

Questions to Explore

  • How could modern cities incorporate principles from forest gardening to create more sustainable food systems?
  • Could lidar technology reveal other large ancient settlements hidden beneath forests around the world?
  • If Maya communities survived and adapted after the decline of major cities, how should this change the way we teach the story of their civilization?
