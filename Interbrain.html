Interbrain Synchrony (Hyperscanning)

What happens inside two brains when people communicate, cooperate, or work toward a shared goal? Discover interbrain synchrony and hyperscanning, research approaches that examine patterns of brain activity across multiple people during social interaction.

Key Concepts

  • Paradigm Shift to Two-Person Neuroscience: Moving away from isolated single-brain experiments, hyperscanning allows cognitive neuroscientists to simultaneously record neural activity from multiple interacting individuals in real time.
  • Interbrain Synchrony and Bidirectional Coupling: Interbrain synchrony measures how brain signals align across individuals during social engagement, treating interacting minds as a dynamic, bidirectionally coupled loop system.
  • Measurement Technologies and Signal Coherence: Using portable tools like electroencephalography and functional near-infrared spectroscopy, researchers map wave alignments using statistical methods like wavelet transform coherence.
  • Cognitive Mechanics and We-Representations: Driven by reciprocal inference and shared intentionality rather than telepathy, interbrain alignment progresses through sensory input and focused attention to build predictive we-representations.
  • Familiarity over Fleeting Closeness: High neural synchrony emerges naturally in parent-child interactions, musical ensembles, and teaching, stemming from long-term shared interaction history rather than artificially induced closeness.

Vocabulary

Synchrony (C2) The state of occurring, moving, or operating at the exact same time or rate, describing aligned neural rhythms between interacting individuals.
Reciprocal (C2) Involving two people or groups who agree to do something similar or give equal assistance to each other, characterizing dynamic social feedback loops.
Coherence (C2) The quality of forming a unified, logical, or mathematical whole, used to calculate statistical correlations between distinct brain wave signals.
Intentionality (C2) The fact or state of being deliberate or done with purpose, underpinning shared cognitive goals between partners.
Artifact (C2) An unintended spurious signal or distortion in data caused by extraneous physical movement rather than cognitive activity.

Big Question

"If interbrain synchrony functions as a shared computational state that dynamically couples human minds during social interaction, how does our physical brain bridge the gap between individual isolation and collective consciousness?"

Questions to Explore

  • How could real-time electroencephalography hyperscanning be implemented in educational classrooms to help teachers dynamically adjust their instructional pacing to student attention?
  • Could therapeutic interventions utilize interbrain synchrony metrics to diagnose or assist individuals facing severe social interaction or communication challenges?
  • What if virtual reality platforms integrated neural alignment algorithms to simulate genuine interpersonal connection across digital communication channels?
