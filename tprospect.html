Prospect Theory

Why does losing money often feel worse than gaining the same amount feels good? Discover Prospect Theory, developed by Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky, and how people evaluate risks, perceive losses, and make decisions under uncertainty.

Key Concepts

  • Multi-Faceted Language Reference Platform: The Cambridge Dictionary operates as a comprehensive online learning hub, integrating monolingual English dictionaries, essential British and American variants, and specialized learner tools.
  • Extensive Translation and Bilingual Resources: The platform features dozens of bilingual and semi-bilingual translation dictionaries spanning global languages, allowing directional switching between English and target tongues.
  • Integrated Grammar and Pronunciation Tools: Users access detailed British grammar guides, audio pronunciation tools, and a dedicated thesaurus to master nuanced written and spoken usage.
  • Interactive Vocabulary Engagement (+Plus and Games): Through the Cambridge Dictionary +Plus platform, users build custom word lists, take interactive quizzes, and play word games like Word Scramble to reinforce vocabulary.
  • Dynamic Lexical Updates and AI Assistance: The platform continuously updates with contemporary terms, daily word highlights, linguistic blogs, and AI-assisted lookup features to reflect evolving language.

Vocabulary

Monolingual (C2) Using or expressed in only one language, describing foundational English definitions.
Bilingual (C2) Using or involving two languages, characterizing translation tools across various language pairs.
Variant (C1) A form of something that differs slightly from other forms of the same type, such as British versus American English definitions.
Retention (C2) The continued use, existence, or keeping of something, describing vocabulary learning goals achieved through interactive games and lists.
Contemporary (C1) Existing or happening in the present time, describing newly added words and modern linguistic trends.

Big Question

"If digital linguistic hubs continuously evolve with artificial intelligence tools, user-generated word lists, and real-time neologism tracking, how does online dictionary infrastructure redefine traditional language learning and global communication?"

Questions to Explore
