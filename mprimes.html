Mersenne Primes

The rare prime numbers hidden within a remarkably simple mathematical formula.

Key Concepts

  • RSA Cryptography: A widely used public-key cryptographic system that relies on mathematical properties of large prime numbers to protect digital communications.
  • Lucas-Lehmer Primality Test: An efficient algorithm used to determine whether numbers of the form 2p − 1 are prime, making it especially important for finding Mersenne primes.
  • Euclid-Euler Theorem: A theorem establishing a direct connection between Mersenne primes and even perfect numbers.
  • Great Internet Mersenne Prime Search: A collaborative distributed-computing project in which computers around the world search for new Mersenne primes.
  • NVIDIA A100 GPU: A powerful graphics processing unit designed for high-performance computing and capable of performing large numbers of calculations efficiently.

Big Question

"If some of the world's digital security depends on the mathematical difficulty of factoring large numbers, how could advances in computing transform the future of internet security?"

Questions to Explore

  • What would happen to modern internet security if a new mathematical method could efficiently factor extremely large numbers?
  • Could the distributed-computing model used to search for Mersenne primes be applied to other large-scale scientific problems?
  • What might the discovery of increasingly large prime numbers reveal about the limits of mathematics and computation?
