History

Feudalism

Explore how feudal societies were organized around land, loyalty, and obligations, and discover how this system shaped medieval European life.

Key Concepts

  • The Feudal Pyramid Myth: Popular education often presents medieval society as a neat pyramid with the king at the top, but medieval political and social relationships were far more complex and varied across regions.
  • Mesne Tenants: These were intermediate landholders who held land from a higher lord while also having their own tenants, creating overlapping relationships of authority and obligation.
  • Commendation Ceremony: This was a formal ceremony through which a person entered a relationship of loyalty and service with a lord, often involving homage and an oath of fealty.
  • Three-Field Crop Rotation: This agricultural system divided farmland into three sections, rotating winter crops, spring crops, and fallow land to improve soil fertility and agricultural productivity.
  • The Bourgeoisie: This urban social class grew alongside medieval commerce and included merchants, artisans, and other people involved in trade and crafts.

Vocabulary

Coercion (C2) The use of force or threats to persuade someone to do something they do not want to do.
Reciprocal (C2) Involving two people or groups who provide similar help, benefits, or obligations to each other.
Fragmentation (C2) The process of breaking something into smaller or separate parts.
Obsolete (C1) No longer used because something newer or more effective has replaced it.
Surplus (C1) An amount that is greater than what is needed or used.

Big Question

"If the feudal system emerged partly as a response to political instability and insecurity, does our modern reliance on centralized governments represent a similar desire for protection and order?"

Questions to Explore

  • How would the power of local lords have changed if gunpowder and effective firearms had appeared several centuries earlier?
  • Could the transition from labor obligations to money payments be considered an important step toward the development of modern market economies?
  • What if medieval cities had not developed independent craft guilds and trading networks? Would feudal society have remained dominant for much longer?
